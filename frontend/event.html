⚙️ How Registration & Waiting List Works

CampusPulse enforces strict capacity limits with an academic-grade FIFO (First-In, First-Out) Queue and O(1) Hash Table duplicate detection:

  • Guaranteed Seats: If seats are available, your registration is instantly CONFIRMED.
  • Fair Waiting List: If the venue is full, you are enqueued into a chronological FIFO waiting list with a verifiable queue rank.
  • Automatic Promotion: The instant any confirmed attendee cancels, the system atomically promotes Queue Candidate #1 to a CONFIRMED seat.
